The Alchemy of AUREUM

Where high-contrast aesthetics meet the fine art of classical perfumery.

The Vision

AUREUM was founded on the philosophy that a fragrance should not simply be worn; it should be felt as a dramatic presence. We blend the absolute darkest, most complex base notes with shimmering elements of light—embodied literally in our signature flakes of 24k gold suspended inside each flask.

By fusing raw ingredients like ancient burning oud, black vetiver, and smoky obsidian minerals with warm gold leaf oils, we create a sensory contrast that shifts, morphs, and deepens over the course of hours.

"To bottle darkness is an art; to make it shine is alchemy."

— Master Perfumer, AUREUM

The Craftsmanship

I

Rare Extraction

Oud and rose petals are steam-distilled using vintage copper alembics to preserve heavy botanical resins.

II

Obsidian Maceration

The oils are matured in darkness for 180 days under thermal volcanic stone containment to enrich woody notes.

III

Gold Suspension

Each flacon receives hand-blown flakes of 24k gold leaf, which slowly release metallic molecules into the solution.
